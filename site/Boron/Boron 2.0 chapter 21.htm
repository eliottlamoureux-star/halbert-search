     Endorsed mailings are the easiest and most simple JV deal you can cut.

     An endorsed mailing is where you get someone to mail to their list personally recommending your wares. If you have the list, it is you that will be recommending someone else’s product.

     The standard deal for information products is a 50/50 split of the gross, after refunds. Each party pays their own expenses. The person with the product delivers it and the other partner delivers the sales.

     JV deals are best done when there is a lot of profit in each unit sold. Information products are what you should stick to.
