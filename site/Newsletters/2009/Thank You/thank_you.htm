Gary Halbert Fans Are The Best

I just want to say thank you to all the fans of my father. Almost every day I hear someone tell me how he's profoundly changed their life forever. It's been a couple of years since he has passed and now it's clear to me that, like the great Claude Hopkins, he will be relevant forever. When I was back in my right mind after his death, I looked back on my dads web site statistics during that time, and I realized my fathers fans went ape shit and downloaded everything on his site.

I really appreciate you die hard true believers, and the applause we get for keeping the site going.I was there in the beginning, my father bribed me with a lot of money to leave my job and start the site. I had to register the site twice because he wanted "The" included in the websites domain name. I stayed up for over three or six weeks, I can't rememeber, working from the moment I woke till I went to bed. I remember he couldn't understand why I needed a very expensive 22" monitor at the time, so I could look at the original Quark file and the web page formatting at the same time.

I'm very proud to have played my small part in something so many of you so strongly believe in..

Some of you write and tell us you can't get enough of my fathers writing. Here is an actual e-mail I received today

Hi Kevin,

It's with regret that your late father only just became a mentor of mine in the last 3 months. I've devoured his Root Canal seminar and Copywriting Bootcamp that were for sale on the internet and I'm stunned at his knowledge and teaching style.

I now find myself in a difficult situation. I discovered his genius too late to take advantage of his live seminars, and yet I actually can't find anything else he's done to buy!

Do you guys have an online store, a series of sales letters or something stashed away that's hard to find with all his materials for sale on? Any assistance in learning more from what has to be one of the best teachers in the history of man on the art and science of marketing and copywriting would be gratefully received.

Thanks
Martin Lightbowne


I have great news for you, when going though our fathers storage unit we found some boxes that contained some of his marketing materials. Bond and I have relented after two years, and finally decided to part with some of the duplicates we have. Before I even finish writing this sentence your asking what the titles of the materials are. To tell you the truth I dont' know what they are, Bond and I just remember there were some duplicates and we decided to let them go first to our fathers fans on the email list.

We are not going to sell every duplicate we have, we are working on compiling the mother of all compilations of my fathers works into one giant package, this by no means will be considered affordable, new cars will sell for less, in this economy that's not saying much.

We may make a fans only section on the site where we'll post what pieces we are releasing, we don't want to turn the site into a circus.

As you know we've been sending you a number of emails endorsing John Carltons Simple Writing System and we wouldn't do that unless we knew he was the next best thing to my father. We intend to bring you new content that we find of value. People ask us all the time if they can contribute to the site, we usually ask them to give us something to post free of charge, that they normally ask money for, in kind we let vistitors know where they can learn more about the contributor.

Earlier in this letter I mentioned the great Claude Hopkins, as most of you probably already know the bulk of my father's marketing philosophy was in lock step with his. Just in case you've never read Claude Hopkins'-Scientific Adversting, as a thank you, I'm making it available right here right now, for FREE in PDF format. His book is now in the public domain and I found it online. When I first read the book I broke out my highlighter, it was of little use, I might as well have dipped the whole book in yellow ink. If anyone has concise list of all the major points of the book I would appreciate a copy.

Peace.

Kevin Halbert
