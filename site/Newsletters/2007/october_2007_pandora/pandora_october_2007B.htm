Starbucks don't sell coffee as much as an experience. This is found by tapping into the deep recesses of your market's psyche and requires a great deal of thought.

4) Story
People like a story, or at least the seeds of one their imagination can pick up on, perhaps by implication. Tom achieves this by implication.
