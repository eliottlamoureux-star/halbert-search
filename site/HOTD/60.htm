Halbertism #60

Tuesday, July 13, 2004

 

If you have a sales pitch on the Internet, NEVER put a live link to a different website in your message.

 

That's crazy. Here you got them interested in YOUR website and reading YOUR message... and then... (like an idiot) you give them a chance to stop reading YOUR message and start reading someone else's.

That's insane.
