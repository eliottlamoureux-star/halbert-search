Halbertism #2

Sunday, May 16, 2004

 

"Nothing is impossible for a person who refuses to listen to reason!"

 

My favorite all time quote. I live by these words.

Reasonable people suck. They never, ever make any type of worthwhile breakthrough. It's the people who REFUSE to listen to reason and conventional wisdom who keep humanity progressing.

These people are my heroes.

Fuck ordinary people.
