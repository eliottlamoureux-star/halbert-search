Why The New Site?

The new site allows us to give people two options on how to view the site. One option is to see the site as Sir Gary originally intended, even more original than it has been in the recent past.

The other option is to see it with it's new additions:

Contributors-The new section will be place where people can hear from contributors that Gary respected.

Store-There will be a place to buy Gary Halbert materials (I know you've been asking for this feature for a long time and now we are finally getting to it).

Affiliate-We will do our best to only introduce you to affiliates that are worth your time and more importantly your bottom line.

Calendar-The calendar section will keep you informed of what's on the horizon and what's happened since the last time you were here.

Bond & Kevin- We will be putting in our two cents (in our own ways), knowing no one's contributions and insites come close to Sir Gary's, particularly in the eyes of his fans.

The site is a work in progress and will continue to evolve. We welcome any constructive and diplomatic suggestions you may have (we can't do everything everyone suggests). Let us know of people you'd like to see contributing to the site or features you think should be added.
